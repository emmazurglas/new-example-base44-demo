About Base44

Building the future of web development, one line of code at a time.

Our Story

Base44 was born in 2020 from a simple observation: developers were spending more time fighting their tools than building great products. We knew there had to be a better way.

What started as a weekend project quickly evolved into a mission to transform how developers work. We've grown from a two-person team working out of a garage to a global community of developers, designers, and innovators.

Today, Base44 powers thousands of applications across 50+ countries, serving millions of users. But we're just getting started. Every day, we're working to make development faster, easier, and more enjoyable.
